# Privacy Notice

**This app only downloads the pages you (or the main-app chat) request.**

## What happens to your data

Every fetch is a direct request from your device to the site you (or the chat) asked for.

Nothing is sent to the app maker or to any third party — no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no accounts.

## The request log

Every requested URL is logged on this app's home screen, so you can always see exactly what was fetched.

The log lives only on your device, and you can delete it at any time with the Delete-all button.

## Open source

This app is fully open source — every line it runs is public and auditable.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will ship inside the app.
